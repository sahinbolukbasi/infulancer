## Rules applied by the system

- **Live check at send time:** IYS is queried before the marketing message; local record is only a pre-filter. If no approval exists, sending is **blocked** and the reason is shown.
- **Mixed content is commercial:** any utility message containing a promotional element (discount, coupon, etc.) also requires approval.
- **Separate IYS connection per brand:** one brand's approval cannot be used for another brand.
- **3 business day SLA:** each approval/rejection record is transmitted to IYS within 3 business days according to the Turkish business day calendar (excluding official holidays and half-day eves). An alarm is generated for any record waiting longer than 1 business day in the queue.
- **Bulk registration:** IYS request is atomic; a single faulty record rejects the entire request (HTTP 422). The system sends 1,000-record batches; if an error occurs, it splits the batch to isolate the faulty record.

## REJECTION is terminal

REJECTION records cannot be deleted or overwritten from the panel, via CSV, or directly in the database. Only a newer-dated and evidenced APPROVAL (form, call record, etc.) can override it. SMS STOP/CANCEL, WhatsApp "stop promotions", Instagram DM, and email unsubscribe requests are recorded as REJECTION through the same workflow. Evidence records are retained for 10 years.

## 6563 md. 12 penalty bands (2026, per recipient)

| Violation | Band |
|---|---|
| Unauthorized message | 2,859 – 14,309 TL |
| Missing sender/content information | 2,859 – 28,620 TL |
| Rejection violation | 5,723 – 42,930 TL |
| Intermediary service provider | 28,620 – 286,206 TL |
| Non-registration / ETBİS | 143,102 – 715,516 TL |

<Callout type="uyari">Penalties are applied **per recipient**: an unapproved campaign to 50,000 recipients is an existential risk for a small company. The 10× multiplier claim in bulk sending has not been verified.</Callout>
