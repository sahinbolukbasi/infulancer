## Rule

- The **first element** of the disclosure must be the literal **"Advertisement"** or **"Promotion"** + the advertiser's name (alternative: "provided by @brand"), **before** all other tags and hashtags.
- **Hashtag alone is not sufficient:** #advertisement, #collaboration, #sponsor can be added but cannot replace the word (hashtag tolerance in the 2021 Guidelines has been invalid since August 1, 2026).
- Must be visible at first glance; cannot be hidden at the end of a long caption.
- **On every frame in a story series** separately. Reels shared to Story carry their own disclosure.

## Platform checklist

| Platform | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Instagram | "Paid partnership" label + disclosure text + top-of-screen text from frame 1 in story/Reels |
| TikTok | Commercial content disclosure ("Your brand" / "Branded content") + text. If missing, video can be removed from For You within 24 hours if not corrected |
| Audio content | Verbal opening **before** the advertisement: "… contains advertisement/promotion" |
| Live / long video | Written **and** verbal |
| YouTube / X | Platform-specific mechanics unverified <Unverified />; general rule applies |

## Our implementation

- Story frame texts, platform labels, and audio content information are entered in the content plan; verification is done **frame by frame**.
- If the advertisement statement is mandatory in the brief, the brief-compliance agent searches for the statement in the draft caption; if absent, it produces a **Warning** finding.
- If non-compliant content is found in campaign publication control, the campaign **cannot be published** (warning, not block).
- At the time of publication, a screenshot/recording of the content must be saved as evidence; correcting afterward does not remove responsibility.

## Penalty

Penalty is **99,339 – 39,916,524 TL** (depending on the medium). In July 2026, two account owners who shared without labeling were each fined **1,083,706 TL** and issued suspension orders.

<Callout type="uyari">The advertiser and influencer can be **penalized separately**; shifting responsibility to the influencer through contract does not remove the advertiser's public law obligation. Two-way indemnification clauses are recommended in contracts.</Callout>
