## Every generated asset

- C2PA Content Credentials are exported **embedded**; which model generated which asset is recorded in the system.
- **Meta:** when creating ads/posts, check the "Created with AI" disclosure. On uploads with C2PA, the "AI Info" label is applied automatically.
- **TikTok:** if there is realistic depiction of a person/place/event, enable the AIGC label; if branded content, also enable the commercial content disclosure.
- Applied together with [md. 23/A](/docs/uyum/reklam-aciklamasi-23a).
- If virtual/AI personas are used, AI disclosure is made separately (article number conflicts among sources <Unverified />).
- EU AI Act md. 50 requires machine-readable marking of synthetic content (in effect since August 2, 2026; systems already on the market have until December 2, 2026).

<Callout type="yasak">Production with an artificial intelligence copy of a real person that creates the impression the person is using or recommending the product (**md. 27/12**) is **always prohibited** — it is not permitted even with a label. If an influencer's name/username in the system, face/voice cloning, or recognizable portrait reference is detected in the brief, the job is rejected.</Callout>

A feature that deletes metadata is **not provided**. Whether fortune outputs carry C2PA has not been tested <Unverified />; the system adds its own manifest in any case.
