## When

The job page shows which stages (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days) are required. **24-hour rule for Stories:** story statistics disappear in 24 hours. A reminder comes 20 hours after posting; you must upload the screenshot by then. A job cannot be completed without a screenshot for Stories.

## Screenshot

- Take a full screen; make sure the date and account name are visible.
- Do not crop, edit, or combine.
- Upload a separate image for each stage.

If the numbers in the form differ by more than 5% from those in the screenshot, the agency will review it.

## My Accounts

Instagram and YouTube follower counts are automatically updated by the agency. For TikTok, you can click "Connect with TikTok" from the **Accounts** page; your password is not shared with us. Since no platform shares audience demographics (age, gender, city), this information is entered from the screenshot.
