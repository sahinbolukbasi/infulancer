## Three Balances

| Balance | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Earned | Work is completed, not yet withdrawable |
| Withdrawable | You can create a withdrawal request |
| In Process | Your open withdrawal requests |

The moment a campaign **ends** (automatically closes after the end date), your completed work earnings move to **withdrawable** balance; you create a withdrawal request from the Payments page, and the request goes to approval in the agency panel. Minimum withdrawal amount is **500 TL**.

## Tax Status

- If you have a **20/B exemption certificate**, payment is made only to the IBAN you specified; the bank deducts 15% withholding tax. The system does not allow payment to another account as it could cause you to lose the exemption.
- If you can issue an invoice/freelance receipt, normal process applies.
- If you cannot issue an invoice, your payment waits until the agency uploads and approves the accountant's opinion.

## Invite

From the **Invite** page, you can create invitation links for other influencers (maximum 5 active links at the same time, maximum 3 people per link, maximum 30 days). Registered users join the platform after agency approval; you receive a notification when they are approved.
