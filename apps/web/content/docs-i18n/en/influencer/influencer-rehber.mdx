## Workflow

1. The agency assigns you to a campaign; you see the brief and content plan on the **My Work** page.
2. You upload your draft (video/image + caption) from the work page.
3. The brand or agency approves or requests revision.
4. After approval, you share at the scheduled time and enter the **share link** on the work page.
5. At the requested stages (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days), you enter your statistics with screenshots.

## Advertising label — mandatory

- Write "Advertisement" or "Promotion" and the brand name at the **very beginning** of the caption. Example: `Advertisement | Lumi Cosmetics — …`
- Only #ad or #collaboration hashtag is **not enough**.
- On Story, you must write "Advertisement | Brand" **on every frame**.
- On Instagram, enable the "Paid partnership" label; on TikTok, enable the "Commercial content" disclosure.
- In audio content, say before the ad: "This content contains advertising about …"

Sharing without a label can result in separate penalties for the advertiser and influencer.

## Artificial intelligence

Content is not produced with artificial intelligence using your face or voice; this is prohibited.
