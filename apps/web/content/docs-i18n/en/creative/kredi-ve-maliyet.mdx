## How it works

1. When production is submitted, an estimated amount is **blocked** from the brand's credit balance (not yet charged).
2. When output arrives successfully, the blocked amount is **charged** and the asset is recorded with C2PA credentials.
3. If production fails, the block is **released**; no fee is charged (fal also does not bill server errors).
4. If the same result comes again (webhook retry), no second charge is made.

## Credit unit

1 credit ≈ $0.05 raw cost assumed; customer price is set at 3–5x markup. Example "ad set package": 6 visuals (Nano Banana Pro) + 8 sec video + ~300 character voiceover ≈ $1.05 raw cost <Unverified />.

## Balance and concurrency

fal prepaid balance also serves as the concurrency limit; in batch production, jobs wait if balance is low. When a brand's balance falls below the low credit threshold, a notification is sent to admin. Account balance, model records, and credit loading are in the "Credits and models" tab on the `/creative` screen. Brands and influencers receive a one-time welcome credit on first login (`CREATIVE_STARTER_CREDITS`, default 30); thereafter credits are loaded by the agency.
