## Setup

A separate number is connected per brand with Embedded Signup **v4** (v2/v3 was deprecated on October 15, 2026). Setup steps: signup → access token → webhook subscription → number registration (2-step PIN) → **brand adds payment method in WhatsApp Manager**. Messages cannot be sent without the final step; this is where most get stuck.

## Message limit and free quota

- **Message limit is at portfolio level** and is shared across all numbers in the portfolio; adding a number does not increase capacity. Tiers: 250 → 2,000 → 10,000 → 100,000 → unlimited (per **unique recipient** in 24 hours).
- **Free 1,000 service messages per number** and are not shared between numbers — this is why each brand gets a separate number.
- Campaigns exceeding the tier are not rejected; the scheduler splits them into days and shows estimated duration.

## 24-hour window

Free messages can be sent within 24 hours after the customer writes. Outside this window, the only compatible outgoing channel is **approved templates**. Conversations from Click-to-WhatsApp ads get 7 days of free messaging. Each conversation in the inbox has a countdown; the agent is notified 15 minutes before closure.

## Template quality

| Quality | System action |
|---|---|
| High | Normal sending |
| Medium | Sending speed reduced to 50%, brand is warned |
| Low | Sending automatically stops, escalated to admin |

1st pause is 3 hours, 2nd pause is 6 hours; on 3rd pause the template is disabled. Categories: marketing / utility / authentication. Review time is not guaranteed <Unverified />.

## Price calendar

Meta price changes only take effect on January 1, April 1, July 1, and October 1 (rate card 1 month, pricing model 3 months, model change 6 months advance notice). Turkey rates have not been verified from Meta's own rate card <Unverified />.
