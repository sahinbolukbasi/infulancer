## Account linking

Facebook Login → Instagram Business/Creator account → permissions: `instagram_basic`, `instagram_manage_insights`, `pages_show_list`. Personal (non-Professional) accounts cannot be linked.

## Automatic follower data retrieval

The agency performs a **Business Discovery** query with its own Instagram Business account: follower count, post count, and average likes/comments of recent posts for public Business/Creator accounts. The influencer does not need to log in. The system does this every 6 hours (every 12 hours for older accounts); it can be triggered manually on the profile with "Sync now". No scraping is performed.

## Available metrics

For linked (own) account: reach, impressions, saves, shares, profile views, follower demographics.

## Story critical note

<Callout type="uyari">Story metrics disappear after 24 hours → **screenshot is mandatory**. The system sends a reminder to the influencer **+20 hours** after posting. On Instagram Story, a job cannot be marked "Completed" without a screenshot metric.</Callout>

## Unavailable

- Detailed demographics of someone else's account (Business Discovery only provides public numbers).
- Archived/hidden metrics.
- Bulk announcements cannot be sent from Instagram (see [Messenger and Instagram DM](/docs/platformlar/messenger-instagram-dm)).
